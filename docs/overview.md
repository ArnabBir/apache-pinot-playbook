# Reading Map

This is a production-minded learning system rather than a linear reference. Use the path that matches the decision in front of you.

## Fast evaluation

Read [Pinot at a Glance](01-apache-pinot-at-a-glance.md), [Architecture](02-architecture-and-components.md), [Indexing](06-indexing-cookbook.md), [Stream Ingestion](08-stream-ingestion.md), and [Patterns](20-patterns-antipatterns-and-decision-framework.md). Then complete [Lab 01](labs/lab-01-local-cluster.md) to see a cluster in motion.

## Building the platform

Begin with [Storage](03-storage-model-segments-tenants-clusters.md), [Schema Design](04-schema-design-and-data-modeling.md), and [Table Configuration](05-table-config-deep-dive.md). Continue through ingestion, deployment, and operations before adapting the capstone to your workload.

## Query engineering

Pair [Indexing](06-indexing-cookbook.md) with [Pinot SQL](10-querying-v1-and-sql.md), [Multi-Stage Engine](11-multi-stage-engine-v2.md), and [Performance Engineering](17-performance-engineering.md). Labs 04, 06, 12, and 16 turn the theory into measurements.

## Operations and reliability

Read [Deployment](14-deployment-docker-kubernetes-cloud.md), [Security](15-security-and-governance.md), [Observability](18-observability-operations-and-minions.md), and [Failure Modes](19-failure-modes-and-troubleshooting.md). Use the labs to rehearse recovery before an incident requires it.

## Complete course

Read chapters in navigation order and complete the labs by phase. Finish with the [Rides Platform Capstone](21-capstone-building-a-rides-platform.md), [Exercises](22-exercises.md), and [Solution Key](23-solution-key.md).
